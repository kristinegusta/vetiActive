Laser Therapy for Dogs and Horses in South Holland

Laser Therapy for Dogs and Horses

Laser therapy is a modern treatment technique that is increasingly used within animal physiotherapy. At 3H Physio, we use laser therapy to support the recovery of animals experiencing pain, injuries, or mobility problems.

We provide laser therapy for dogs and horses in South Holland, including the regions of Gouda, Leiden, Rotterdam, and Utrecht.

Laser therapy uses concentrated light energy to stimulate the body's natural healing processes. The treatment is non-invasive, painless, and safe, and can help reduce inflammation, relieve pain, and promote tissue repair.

Because laser therapy works on a cellular level, it can support recovery after injuries, surgeries, or chronic conditions.

How Does Laser Therapy Work?

During a laser treatment, light with a specific wavelength penetrates the tissue. This process stimulates the cells and increases energy production within the cell.

This can lead to:

  • Improved blood circulation
  • Reduction of inflammation
  • Stimulation of tissue repair
  • Pain relief
  • Improved mobility

Laser therapy is often used as part of a rehabilitation program, together with physiotherapy exercises and manual therapy.

When Is Laser Therapy Used?

Laser therapy can be applied for various orthopedic and neurological conditions in animals.

Common indications include:

  • Back and neck problems
  • Muscle injuries
  • Tendon and ligament injuries
  • Osteoarthritis and joint pain
  • Nerve-related problems
  • Rehabilitation after surgery (for example after spinal surgery or cruciate ligament surgery)
  • Wound healing and scar tissue

By combining laser therapy with animal physiotherapy, the recovery process can be supported and mobility may improve.

Laser Therapy for Dogs

Laser therapy is commonly used in dogs with:

  • Back problems or herniated discs
  • Muscle and tendon injuries
  • Joint conditions such as osteoarthritis
  • Recovery after orthopedic surgery
  • Chronic pain conditions

The treatment can help reduce pain and accelerate recovery, allowing dogs to move more comfortably again.

Laser Therapy for Horses

Laser therapy can also be a valuable addition to equine rehabilitation.

In horses, laser therapy is often used for:

  • Muscle problems
  • Tendon and ligament injuries
  • Back problems
  • Joint inflammation
  • Recovery after injuries or intensive training

The treatment can improve blood circulation and stimulate the healing of damaged tissue.
